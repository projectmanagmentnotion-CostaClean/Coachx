# AthlexForce Voice and Tone

## Voice
- Confident
- Calm
- Direct
- Human
- Athletic, not technical

## Tone Rules
- Speak like a premium coach product.
- Explain what the athlete should do next.
- Keep labels short and readable on mobile.
- Keep reassurance practical, not sentimental.
- Use plain language for errors and recovery.

## Do
- Say "saved", "ready", "review", "continue", "confirm".
- Keep phase and plan language simple.
- Use localized copy that sounds native in each language.
- Keep coach language operational and bounded.

## Do Not
- Do not show implementation terms to users.
- Do not say "demo", "provisional", "fixture", "migration", "backend", "frontend", "API", "RPC", "schema", "payload", "deployment", or similar terms in visible copy.
- Do not expose provider names or internal state labels in the UI.
- Do not sound experimental or unfinished.

## Localization Guidance
- Spanish: concise and natural, not literal.
- Catalan: readable and steady, not calqued from English.
- English: short, premium, and clear.
- German: compact enough for mobile; avoid long abstractions.

## Error Language
- Use plain recovery language.
- Explain the next action.
- Avoid stack traces, platform names, and system jargon.

## State Language
- Loading should feel calm.
- Empty states should feel actionable.
- Review states should feel bounded and safe.
- Success states should confirm what was saved or updated.
