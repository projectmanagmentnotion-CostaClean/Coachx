## Practical Takeaways

- Premium motion should feel restrained and product-led.
- Motion should be layered, not constant.
- Overlays need explicit viewport and stacking rules.
- Mobile legibility matters more than decorative animation.
- Reduced-motion behavior must remain first-class.
