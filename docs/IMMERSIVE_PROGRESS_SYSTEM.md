# Immersive Progress System

## Purpose

The immersive progress system turns existing progress, adherence, and phase-review data into a clear motivational surface without inventing fake urgency or achievements.

## Principles

- Use real data only.
- Reward progress with restraint.
- Keep the visual language warm, not noisy.
- Preserve calm states when the athlete is not near a concrete target.
- Never imply health, medical, or performance guarantees.

## Surfaces

- `Performance Analytics` hero and charts
- `Progress Trends` summary and chart context
- `Phase Review` achievement moment

## Core Model

- `state`: calm, active, close, heat, achieved
- `primaryTarget`: the most relevant live metric
- `targets`: the active metrics currently being tracked
- `milestones`: real completions that deserve acknowledgement

## What Counts as Immersive

- Progress ring with target proximity
- Warm chart target lines
- Achievement chips when the data is actually complete
- Small motion cues that reinforce momentum

## What Does Not Count

- Fake streak pressure
- False urgency
- Random celebration
- Health shaming
- Placeholder progress framed as success

## Data Inputs

- Training adherence
- Nutrition adherence
- Hydration
- Phase completion
- Workout load milestones

## Accessibility

- Respect reduced motion.
- Keep text readable at mobile widths.
- Do not rely on animation to convey the data.
